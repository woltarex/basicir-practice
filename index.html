      Interception

      Δ = angle between the radial you are on and the required radial.

      |Δ|TypeTracks
      < 15°Correctionrequired track ± min(2·|Δ|, 30°)
      15°–30°45°required track ± 45°
      30°–70°90°/45°initial ± 90°, then interception ± 45°
      > 70° (outbound)Over-stationtrack to the station, then radial ± min(round(|s| / 3), 30°)

      s = difference between the track to the station and the required radial.

      Holding entry

      STD: d = track to fix − inbound. Non-STD: d = inbound − track to fix.

      • 0–70° and 290–360°: Direct — turn to the outbound track
      • 70–110°: Special direct — hold your track 15 s, turn to outbound, 45 s
      • 110–180°: Teardrop — outbound ∓ 30° (STD −30°, Non-STD +30°)
      • 180–290°: Parallel — fly the outbound track on the non-holding side
      • Within ±5° of 110°, 180° or 290°: Either of the two neighbouring entries
